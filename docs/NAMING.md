# FolioFlux — Naming Research & Decision Record

## 1. Chosen Name
**FolioFlux** (Display name: **FolioFlux PDF**)

## 2. Etymology & Brand Identity
- **Folio**: From the Latin *folium* (leaf, sheet of paper), historically denoting a page, a manuscript leaf, or a standard book size. It immediately anchors the product in documents, pages, and typography.
- **Flux**: Denoting continuous flow, fluidity, responsiveness, and dynamism. It represents lightning-fast page rendering, effortless transitions between viewing and editing, and smooth document transformation.
- **Combined Impact**: "FolioFlux" conveys a modern, lightweight, dynamic, and fluid document workspace that makes reading and editing PDFs feel natural, responsive, and delightful rather than sluggish or intimidating.
